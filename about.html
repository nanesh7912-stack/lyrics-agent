About the project

Music deserves words that move with it.

AI Lyrics Hero is a lyric-video creation project built around one simple idea: the words on screen should feel like they belong to the song.

The idea

Keep the human part at the center.

A song carries its own pace, texture, and emotion. AI Lyrics Hero brings lyrics and audio together, helping creators turn that combination into a video with readable captions and music-led visuals.

The project is designed to make a strong starting point, while leaving creative choices with the artist. Your lyric text stays in control of what appears on screen, and timing can be reviewed and adjusted for the performance.

AI Lyrics Hero is an evolving creator tool. This website introduces the project and its capabilities; it does not process uploads or create videos in the browser.
