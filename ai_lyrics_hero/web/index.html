HERE TO HELP

Help center

A few quick answers to get your lyric video looking right.

Can I make a video without a lyrics file?

Yes. Choose Auto-detect and the video engine will find matching catalog lyrics or transcribe the song. You can review and correct the words before the video renders. Singing can be hard to recognize, so listen to the marked words.

How do I get more accurate lyric timing?

Automatic word timing is created from speech recognition. For exact timing, provide timestamped LRC lyrics. The downloadable review report includes the timing used for each line.

Does the video cut or loop my song?

No. The selected section plays continuously for the full requested duration. If the selected duration goes beyond the audio, the remaining part is silent.

Where are my projects saved?

Project settings are saved to the service and attached to your private browser session. Use the same browser on the same device to see them again.

What files can I upload?

MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, FLAC, OGG, OPUS, WMA, and MP4 audio files up to 100 MB.

ABOUT THE STUDIO

Songs deserve to be seen

AI Lyrics Hero turns the words in your music into a video you can share.

♫

Made for the feeling behind the lyrics

AI Lyrics Hero is a browser-based lyric video studio. Add a song, create a first-pass transcript automatically or bring your own lyrics, and render a vertical video with timed words and moving visual styles.

The goal is to make the creative workflow approachable: no editing timeline to learn, no local video software to install, and an editable timing report alongside each finished video.
