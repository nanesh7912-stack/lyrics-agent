Put your own images here (they are NOT part of the project and are never uploaded anywhere):

  assets/user/backgrounds/<scene>/*.jpg|png|webp   full-screen photos/illustrations for a scene, e.g.
        backgrounds/moon/night1.jpg, backgrounds/city/skyline.png, backgrounds/dream/clouds.jpg
        (scene names: moon eye heart_fire rain romance city road nature galaxy stars dream night sky streaks)
  assets/user/icons/<entry id>.png                 transparent PNGs that replace the emoji picture, e.g. car.png

Optional: a LICENSES.txt next to the images; its first lines are copied into the render report.
Images are cover-cropped to the video's aspect ratio, darkened slightly so lyrics stay readable, and given a
slow zoom/pan. Only png/jpg/jpeg/webp up to 25 MB are read; unreadable files are skipped with a warning.
Use images you have the right to use. Nothing is downloaded from the internet.
